Because, but, and so

妈妈本周只抓：用连接词讲清关系
每周三次：A 看＋练 15–20 分；B 读＋答 15–20 分；C 写＋改 20–25 分。

先看视频：How to Write a Sentence for Kids

Ms. Dorismond's Virtual Corner · about 4 minutes
妈妈只需知道：看完整句：说清谁或什么，以及发生了什么。

ONE IDEA TO LEARN

Because introduces a reason. But shows a contrast. So introduces a result. Choose the word that matches your meaning.

WORKED EXAMPLE

The floor was wet, so Ana walked slowly.
Ana walked slowly because the floor was wet.
Both connect the same cause and result.

A / B 核对答案

A1：but
看点：前后有对比：盖子松，但她把它扶住；so 也可表达结果，不过本题选项是 because/but。

A2：No pieces fell out because the lid was secured.

B1：They stopped because the lid was loose.
看点：原因是盖子松。

B2：They secured the lid, so no pieces fell out.
看点：先行动，再结果；不能颠倒成没有掉落所以才固定。

卡住只说这一段

画“盖松 → 停下固定 → 没掉零件”三个框。让他指一对框，先口头说关系再写。

C参考答案（措辞不同也可以）

The children stopped because the lid was loose. Maya and Leo secured it before moving the box. The lid stayed in place, so the small pieces did not fall out.

中文意思 / 接受标准：有停下的原因，也有固定后的结果。不要要求两种连接词出现在同一句里。

ONE IDEA TO LEARN

Use a name when he, she, or they could confuse the reader. In a story about the past, keep your verbs in the past unless the meaning changes.
